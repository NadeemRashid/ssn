Privacy Policy

SSN Care Solutions

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

At SSN Care Solutions, we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of personal information, especially concerning the vulnerable adults, clients, caregivers, and families we support. Because our management team consists of qualified social workers and CQC registered care managers, we understand and enforce high standards of confidentiality, care ethics, and data protection.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process personal data in order to plan and deliver specialized deep cleaning services safely. This may include:

  • Contact details (Name, address, phone number, email address).
  • Support and Access Needs (Details regarding dementia, learning disabilities, mental health conditions, self-neglect, or hoarding parameters required to carry out safe cleaning).
  • Care team details (Names of social workers, care coordinators, guardians, or family representatives).
  • Billing and transaction details (Details about service payments and quotes).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • Planning client-specific cleaning solutions that align with care priorities.
  • Coordinating safety measures and accessing facilities alongside guardians or care authorities.
  • Ensuring proper compliance with safeguarding protocols and infection control parameters.
  • Communication, billing, and responding to scheduling inquiries.

3. Data Sharing and Confidentiality

Confidentiality is a fundamental priority for our qualified social work team. We will never sell or rent client personal information. We only share information when required under the following circumstances:

  • Safeguarding & Legal Requirements: If we discover immediate risks to a client's safety or health, we have a professional duty to alert local safeguarding teams or healthcare managers.
  • Service Coordination: Sharing essential details with CQC care managers or local authority social workers actively coordinating the client's home care parameters.

4. Security & Retention

We deploy robust digital and organizational security measures to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of personal care records. Client information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the service purpose or meet clinical records retention requirements.

5. Your Legal Rights

Under GDPR, clients or their legal guardians have the right to request access to personal data, request corrections, or request deletion of personal records under certain conditions. To execute any rights, please contact our management team.
