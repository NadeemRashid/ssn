Care & Support Priorities

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

SSN Care Solutions was founded specifically to bridge the gap between commercial deep cleaning and professional social care. We combine advanced sanitation techniques with the sensitivity, dignity, and safety regulations required to support vulnerable adults in independent living facilities, assisted spaces, or their own homes.

Qualified Professional Leadership

Unlike standard cleaning services, our senior management team is comprised of qualified Social Workers and CQC registered Care Managers. This unique expertise ensures that every deep clean we coordinate is approached through a care planning lens, taking into account client capacity, safeguarding parameters, dementia-friendly guidelines, and social service priorities.

Aligning with CQC Quality Statements

We train our staff and structure our protocols to align with key Care Quality Commission (CQC) principles for safe and effective care environments:

1. Safe Environments: We address infection risks, cross-contamination, pest control preparation, and severe accumulation hazards to eliminate health risks. We restore environments to safe, sanitary, and functional conditions that prevent accidents.

2. Caring with Dignity: For individuals dealing with hoarding behavior or self-neglect, decluttering is highly stressful. We employ compassionate communication, working at the client’s pace without forcing disposal, and preserving essential personal papers and sentimental items.

3. Responsive to Support Needs: We collaborate directly with families, guardians, NHS discharge coordinators, and local authorities to create personalized schedules that align with post-discharge plans or existing support packages.

Safeguarding Vulnerable Adults

All cleaning technicians are fully DBS-checked, reference-vetted, and receive training on adult safeguarding protocols (Section 42 criteria of the Care Act). Our social work management team handles assessments and reviews, ensuring that if our staff spot any signs of abuse, rapid physical deterioration, or unmanaged care needs, we immediately report to the responsible care team or safeguarding authorities.

Infection Control & Clinical Cleans

Our disinfection protocols meet strict infection control guidelines. We utilize non-toxic, eco-friendly hospital-grade sanitizers that kill 99.9% of bacteria and viruses, keeping homes safe for clients with respiratory conditions or compromised immune systems.

A Trusted Partner for Care Organizations

We work as a reliable partner for NHS Trusts, housing associations, community mental health teams, and registered care agencies, delivering clean homes that allow care workers to safely perform their daily duties.
