Our Story & Philosophy

Bridging the gap between clinical cleaning and social care support.

SSN Care Solutions was created by two ambitious, hardworking individuals who work in the care sector and saw an opportunity to make a real difference to the lives of vulnerable adults.

Our unique selling point is that our senior management team is made up of qualified social workers and CQC registered care managers. We therefore understand the needs of those receiving care and support whilst living in their own homes.

These include those who may have needs such as dementia, learning disability, and mental health conditions leading to hoarding and self-neglect.

At SSN Care Solutions, we understand that maintaining a clean and safe environment is crucial in any setting. Whether it's your own home, a commercial property, or an assisted living facility, our dedicated team of cleaning professionals is committed to providing exceptional cleaning services tailored to meet the unique needs of your facility.

Our Offerings

Specialist Care & Deep Cleaning Services

Tailored support services that ensure a clean, hygienic, and safe environment for you or your loved ones.

Daily & Weekly Cleaning

Regular maintenance, light housekeeping, dusting, vacuuming, and general upkeep on a schedules plan that suits your routine.

Deep Cleaning & Sanitization

A rigorous, top-to-bottom scrub down of kitchens, bathrooms, high-touch points, and baseboards to eliminate persistent dirt.

Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

Eco-friendly, deep-steam extraction to remove embedded dust, dirt, allergens, and stains from carpets, sofas, and fabrics.

Window Washing

Streak-free interior and exterior window glass cleaning to maximize natural light and improve the layout ambience.

Restroom Sanitation

Disinfecting procedures utilizing medical-grade sanitizing solutions on all restroom fixtures, tile, and flooring.

Infection Control Cleaning

Specialized decontamination to neutralize viruses and bacteria, creating a clinically safe home environment for vulnerable individuals.

Hospital to Home Transition

Post-Discharge Deep Cleaning

Rapid response deep cleaning to prepare homes for individuals returning from hospital stays. We prioritize safety, infection control, and access parameters, making the home environment completely ready and supportive for immediate recovery.
