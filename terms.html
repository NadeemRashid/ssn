1. Provision of Services

SSN Care Solutions provides specialized deep cleaning, decluttering, sanitizing, and supportive clear-out services. Since we specialize in helping vulnerable adults, dementia patients, and individuals suffering from self-neglect or hoarding, our service parameters are coordinated with care requirements and are led by qualified social workers and CQC registered care managers.

2. Bookings, Assessment, and Quotes

Every assignment begins with a detailed initial assessment. Quotes are calculated based on the home's condition, safety hazards, access conditions, and clinical parameters. We reserve the right to modify the initial quote if the assessment details provided by the client/representative were incomplete or if unforeseen biohazards are discovered upon arrival.

3. Access & Safety Parameters

The health, safety, and emotional well-being of both our clients and staff are paramount. We require:

  • Safe and clear access to the property at the scheduled times.
  • Disclosure of any structural hazards, pest issues, or active biohazards before services begin.
  • Our staff reserve the right to pause operations if they encounter immediate physical threats, verbal abuse, or severe unmanaged safety hazards.

4. Cancellations and Rescheduling

We understand that circumstances regarding social care and hospital discharge transitions change rapidly. We require at least 48 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made less than 48 hours prior to service may incur a late-notice booking fee to offset team deployment scheduling.

5. Disposal and Sorting Protocols

For hoarding or severe self-neglect clear-outs, our social care managers work carefully with the client or representative to sort belongings. No significant possessions or papers will be disposed of without explicit consent from the client or their legal representative/guardian, unless they pose direct health or structural safety hazards that have been pre-arranged for removal.
